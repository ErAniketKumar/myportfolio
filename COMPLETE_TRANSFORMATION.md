```css

Medium Dark: #1f2937 (gray-800)
Text Primary: #ffffff (white)
Text Secondary: #9ca3af (gray-400)
Border: #374151 (gray-700)
```

### Typography

- **Font Family**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: Bold, Gradient text
- **Body**: Regular, Gray-300/400
- **Highlights**: Golden, Semibold

### Spacing

- Sections: py-20 (80px)
- Cards: p-8 to p-12
- Gaps: 6-8 units
- Max Width: 7xl (1280px)

### Animations

- **Entrance**: Fade + Slide (0.6-0.8s)
- **Hover**: Scale (1.02-1.05) + Border glow
- **Stagger**: 0.1-0.2s delay
- **Smooth**: All transitions
